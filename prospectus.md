
# Windsmere University — Prospectus (Training Resource)

## Welcome
Windsmere University is presented here as a training resource to teach website-building, course design, and higher-education communications. The contents are illustrative and for classroom use.

## Study Areas (selected)
- Computer Science & AI
- Quantum Computing
- Robotics & Autonomous Systems
- Cybersecurity & Digital Forensics
- Synthetic Biology & Biodesign
- Renewable Energy & Environmental Science
- Business & Innovation

## Sample Programmes
**BSc Computer Science (Hons)** — 3 years. Core modules: Programming, Systems, Algorithms, Databases.
**BSc AI & Ethics** — 3 years. Core modules: Machine Learning, Ethics & Governance, Human-Centred AI.
**MSc Quantum Computing** — 1 year. Core modules: Quantum Algorithms, Quantum Error Correction, Hardware Interfaces.
**MSc Autonomous Systems & Robotics** — 1 year. Core modules: Perception, Control, Embedded Systems.
**BSc Synthetic Biology** — 3 years. Core modules: Molecular Biology, Lab Practice, Biodesign (lab-based).

## Admissions (typical)
- Undergraduate: A-levels or equivalent; personal statement; reference.
- Postgraduate: Bachelor's degree in a relevant field; references; statement of purpose.
- International: English language proof (e.g., IELTS/TOEFL) may be required; consult official guidance for visa information.

## Campus & Facilities
- Great Library of Northshire — Special collections and study spaces.
- Innovation Hub — Labs, makerspaces, and startup support.
- Lakeside Student Village — Accommodation and wellbeing services.

## Research & Industry Links
Windsmere promotes applied research and partnerships with industry on renewable energy, AI safety, and biotech.
