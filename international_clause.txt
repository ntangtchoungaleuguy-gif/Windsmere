
International Students — Clause (Training Resource)

Windsmere welcomes international applicants. As a training resource, Windsmere provides the following illustrative guidance:

- Applicants should hold qualifications equivalent to UK entry standards.
- English language proficiency (such as IELTS/TOEFL) may be required; typical thresholds vary by programme.
- Applicants should consult official government guidance for visa requirements and proof-of-funds rules; this site does not provide legal immigration advice.
- International applicants may be asked to provide certified translations of academic documents and attend pre-arrival orientation.
- The university (training resource) does not process visas or issue immigration sponsorship documents; real institutions and official sources should be consulted for immigration sponsorship and visa support.
