
Admissions Requirements (Summary)

Undergraduate (UK):
- Typically 3 A-levels or equivalent in relevant subjects (e.g., Mathematics for computing).
- Personal statement outlining motivation and relevant experience.
- One academic reference.
- Portfolios or auditions for arts and performance programmes.

Undergraduate (International):
- Recognised secondary qualification equivalent to UK A-levels/IB.
- Official transcripts and translations where applicable.
- English language proficiency evidence (IELTS/TOEFL) if required.
- Personal statement and reference(s).

Postgraduate (Taught):
- Bachelor's degree (2:1 or equivalent) in a relevant field.
- Academic references and a statement of purpose.
- For technical programmes, evidence of programming/math competence may be requested.

Research Degrees:
- Relevant postgraduate qualifications, research proposal, supervisor match, and references.
- Interviews and/or presentations may be required.

Assessment & Interviews:
- Some programmes use technical tests, coding assessments, or interviews for selection.
- Lab-based programmes require agreement to lab safety training and compliance with safety protocols.
